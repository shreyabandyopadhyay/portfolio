Hello! I'm Shreya, a Business Systems and Data Analyst with nearly six years of experience delivering data-driven solutions within the banking and financial services industry. Throughout my career, I've worked on large-scale customer analytics, data warehousing, enterprise data platforms, and intelligent document processing initiatives for leading financial institutions. I specialize in transforming complex business requirements into practical data solutions that improve decision-making, operational efficiency, and reporting accuracy.

My expertise spans business analysis, data analytics, and enterprise data management. I have extensive experience in requirements elicitation, stakeholder collaboration, source-to-target mapping, data profiling, reconciliation, impact analysis, and User Acceptance Testing (UAT). Working closely with business teams, data engineers, and technology stakeholders, I help ensure that data solutions align with business objectives while maintaining accuracy, scalability, and governance standards.

Over the years, I've built strong technical capabilities in SQL, AWS Redshift, IBM Netezza, Power BI, Python, and Microsoft Power Platform. My work has involved analyzing millions of customer and account records, supporting data transformation initiatives, implementing data quality controls, and enabling meaningful insights through analytics and reporting. I enjoy uncovering patterns in data, solving complex business problems, and creating solutions that drive measurable business value.

In addition to my professional experience, I have a strong academic foundation in computer science, machine learning, and artificial intelligence. This combination of business analysis, data expertise, and emerging technology allows me to approach challenges from both strategic and technical perspectives.

I am passionate about bridging the gap between business needs and technology solutions. Whether it's optimizing data processes, supporting enterprise transformation initiatives, or leveraging analytics to drive smarter decisions, I am always motivated by opportunities to create meaningful impact through data and collaboration.
