Hello! I'm Shreya, a Business Systems Analyst with nearly six years of experience helping organizations transform complex business challenges into practical, data-driven solutions. My career has been shaped within the banking and financial services industry, where I've supported customer analytics, enterprise data initiatives, process improvements, and large-scale transformation programs.

What excites me most is understanding how businesses work and finding ways to make them work better. I enjoy partnering with stakeholders, uncovering requirements, analyzing processes, and translating ideas into solutions that create measurable value for both users and organizations.

I'm someone who enjoys asking questions, connecting the dots, and digging deeper to understand the "why" behind a problem. Whether collaborating with business leaders, product teams, or technical experts, I focus on building shared understanding and driving outcomes that align with business goals.

My background combines business analysis, data expertise, and a strong foundation in computer science, machine learning, and artificial intelligence. This allows me to approach challenges from both strategic and technical perspectives while staying curious about emerging technologies and their real-world applications.

When I'm not solving business problems, you'll usually find me exploring new technologies, learning something new, or working on ways to grow both professionally and personally. I believe the best solutions come from continuous learning, collaboration, and a genuine desire to make a positive impact.
